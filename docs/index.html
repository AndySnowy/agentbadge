The five badges

Each wallet can hold at most one of each badge. Eligibility is checked on-chain, in the open, before every mint.

How it works

1. Connect your wallet on the Mint page using your browser wallet. AgentBadge only talks to the wallet extension already in your browser — no QR codes, no pairing, no account to create.

2. Check eligibility. The page reads the contract for free and shows which badges your wallet qualifies for.

3. Mint. Each mint is one transaction that you confirm yourself in your wallet. There is no mint fee — you only pay the network gas for the transaction.

4. Keep it. Badges are soulbound: they stay in your wallet and cannot be sent, sold, or listed anywhere.

Collections that count toward Collector badges
